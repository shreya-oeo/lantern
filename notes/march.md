# march, somewhere

Bought the cheap notebook again. Every time I say I'll finish the one before it
and every time I start a new one, which is maybe the whole problem, or maybe the
thing I actually like about notebooks.

We drove out past the reservoirs to look at the water. Nobody talked for about
forty minutes and it was the best conversation of the month. I want to write
that down before I decide it means something.

The radio only worked when we were going downhill.

I keep a list of things I'd do if I had a free Tuesday. It's four years long and
I have never once had a free Tuesday.
