# october, late

Grandma's recipe card is in her handwriting and I can't read two of the words.
I keep the card in the tin with the others and take it out every year and don't
make anything.

I have started making the soup. I don't know if I'm doing it right. It tastes
like the house, which is not the same as tasting like her, but it's the closest
thing available, and I would like to be honest that it's fine.

I want to be the person in the family who knows how to make the soup. That is
currently the whole ambition.

The card has a grease stain shaped like a thumb over the word "enough".
